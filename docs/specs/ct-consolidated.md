# Part I — Architecture

## 1. Positions

Eight positions, derived from a four-letter MBTI type. Four ego positions carry the functions the person identifies with; four shadow positions carry the same base functions in opposing attitude. Augusta's Model A classifications (Strong/Weak × Valued/Unvalued) are retained because they explain why Counter and Critic operate proficiently without any activation mechanism.

| Pos | Name | Arc | Augusta | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead | Ego | Strong + Valued | Primary perception; directable input channel; the function the self most identifies with |
| 2 | Anchor | Ego | Strong + Valued | Evaluative standard; the gate Lead's output must pass for execution; conscious stabilizer |
| 3 | Refuge | Ego | Weak + Valued | Engine: forward motion in active mode, restoration in rest mode |
| 4 | Hunger | Ego | Weak + Valued | Motivational base; constitutively gap-shaped; the seat of the fixation |
| 5 | Counter | Shadow | Strong + Unvalued | Lead's attitude flipped; defends motivated striving; proficient but not identified-with |
| 6 | Critic | Shadow | Strong + Unvalued | Anchor's attitude flipped; samples Refuge's unguarded behavior and updates Anchor's standard |
| 7 | Gamble | Shadow | Weak + Unvalued | Refuge's attitude flipped; discrepancy detector between Flood's background stream and awareness |
| 8 | Flood | Shadow | Weak + Unvalued | Hunger's attitude flipped; always-on background input; emergency override |

**Derivation rule.** Dominant → Lead; auxiliary (opposite attitude) → Anchor; tertiary (dominant's attitude) → Refuge; inferior (opposite of tertiary) → Hunger. Flip each ego function's attitude in order to obtain Counter, Critic, Gamble, Flood. The full table is Appendix A. Every type's shadow stack is another type's ego stack.

## 2. Two pairings, kept distinct

Confusing these is the most common contamination error.

**Domain pairs** share a base function in opposing attitudes: Lead/Counter (1–5), Anchor/Critic (2–6), Refuge/Gamble (3–7), Hunger/Flood (4–8). A domain pair is one functional territory split across two access profiles. Colonization must capture both members to seal a domain, and it reaches them separately; this positional protection is why Terminal arrives in four stages rather than two.

**Nested pairs** are the dependency couplings: Lead/Flood (1–8), Anchor/Gamble (2–7), Refuge/Critic (3–6), Hunger/Counter (4–5). Each shadow position is built to read its nested partner's output — Counter monitors Hunger, Critic samples Refuge, Gamble reads Anchor's standard, Flood evaluates its trigger on Lead-filtered perception. The colonization sequence runs on the nested pairs. "Mirror partner" in this document always means nested partner.

## 3. The nested structure

```
Lead (1)       directable input
  Anchor (2)     evaluative gate
    Refuge (3)     engine
      Hunger (4)     fixation site
      Counter (5)    fixation defense
    Critic (6)     calibration
  Gamble (7)     sonar
Flood (8)      always-on background input
```

Two flows run in opposite directions. Reality enters at the outer poles: Lead's input passes through Anchor's gate; Flood's input feeds the background and surfaces, when it does, through Gamble. The fixation sits at the center and corrupts outward. Terminal is when both poles are captured and the system is a closed loop.

## 4. The two input channels

Lead and Flood share the person's E/I attitude and are both perceiving-or-judging in the same direction the person faces. Lead is attended, directed, and identified-with. Flood is unattended, continuous, and disowned. Flood is not an eruption; it runs all the time. It becomes visible only when it surfaces through Gamble or when it takes over under extreme stress, and it tells you as much about the stack as Lead does. Behavioral signatures the folk model attributes to Lead are frequently Flood — the ENFP's Se-Flood produces the sensory immersion that gets read as "Ne enthusiasm."

Two distinct stress events use these positions. **Hunger Reaching** (moderate stress): the inferior function attempts to operate without practiced capacity — exaggerated, unskilled, recognizable as the "inferior grip." **Flood forced-primary** (extreme stress, Lead offline): the background channel becomes the only input; the person does not reach, they are submerged. Same base function, opposing attitudes, distinguishable by direction, quality, and severity.

## 5. The engine

Refuge translates Lead's perception and Anchor's evaluation into daily forward motion, and restores capacity at rest through engagement with its own function. Types sharing a Refuge function share a propulsion texture regardless of Lead: Te-Refuge (ENFP, ESFP) dispatches problems for efficiency; Si-Refuge (INFP, INTP) makes somatic micro-adjustments; Fe-Refuge (ENTP, ESTP) works the emotional field; Ni-Refuge (ISTP, ISFP) orients by periodic convergent insight; Ne-Refuge (ESTJ, ESFJ) discovers possibility inside established routine; Fi-Refuge (ISTJ, INTJ) aligns the practical life to values invisibly; Ti-Refuge (INFJ, ISFJ) builds analytical structure under the relational surface; Se-Refuge (ENTJ, ENFJ) delivers with physical command. The engine is independent of the steering wheel; when it surfaces it contradicts the Lead-based type image.

Refuge is the first ego position corrupted after Hunger, because it is adjacent to Counter and its charge — both propulsion and recovery — is what Counter's threat output contaminates.

## 6. The sonar

Gamble is the evaluative gateway between Flood's stream and awareness. It surfaces background data when that data conflicts with the standard the system holds; the conscious experience has Gamble's functional signature, not Flood's. It cannot be summoned because the background channel cannot be queried. It has three properties, developed in Part II §6: **sensitivity** (the threshold a signal must cross; feedback-modulated), **lens** (the function it evaluates through; Refuge's in opposing attitude), and **polarity** (the reference it compares against; Anchor's standard). It is the registration organ for the growth mechanism (Part IV §1), which makes it the hinge of the clinical half of this document.

## 7. Enneatype and instinctual variant

The fixation is the constitutional shape of Hunger. Its **structure** — the basic fear/desire architecture — is inborn. Its **calibration** — the scarcity model: how bad, how likely, how soon, in which domain — is learned. Counter's threat output is constitutional structure running on learned parameters; growth work revises the parameters, never the structure. Type does not change over a lifetime; level does. Falsifier: monozygotic twins raised apart should show high concordance on type position *and wing*; if concordance is no higher than dizygotic, the structural claim is wrong.

Type position is a point on a continuous circle (Riso). Wing is the angular precision of that point, not a separate variable, and cannot shift independently of core type. Apparent wing shifts are function development, level change, or IV expression.

Instinctual variants are grounded in Panksepp's primary content drives — SP in CARE, SO in PLAY, SX in LUST — and are the best-grounded component of the thumbprint. The first instinct's territory is where **substrate pressure** arises: IV-domain threat (material stability for SP-first, group standing for SO-first, dyadic intensity for SX-first). Substrate pressure is present from birth, sets the amplitude of the fixation signal at Hunger, and is the rate term on every accumulation capture in Part II. It is not the fixation and it is not the level; it is what drives the level.

Level 1 — no position captured — is therefore not a developmental state anyone starts in. It is reachable only by recovery, which matches Riso-Hudson's Liberation as earned rather than innate.

# Part II — Mechanism

## 1. Adjacency corruption

One mechanism: **a shadow position's corrupted output becomes an ego position's input, and sustained corrupted input recalibrates the receiver.** Critic write-back — Critic samples Refuge's unguarded behavior and updates Anchor's standard — is the middle instance of this rule, and "write-back" remains its name. It is not the whole mechanism.

Two clauses generate the sequence.

**Ego → shadow.** Each ego position corrupts its nested partner: Hunger→Counter, Refuge→Critic, Anchor→Gamble, Lead→Flood. The shadow position reads its partner's output to do its own job, so when the partner's output becomes fixation-shaped the shadow position follows.

**Shadow → ego.** Each shadow position corrupts the ego position one step *outward* from its nested partner: Counter(5)→Refuge(3), Critic(6)→Anchor(2), Gamble(7)→Lead(1). Flood's outward target would be position 0, which does not exist. The sequence closes at Flood structurally, not because severity ran out.

Alternate the clauses from Hunger and the order is 4, 5, 3, 6, 2, 7, 1, 8. The alternation is not a rhythm imposed on the sequence; it is the wiring. Ego positions are parallel consumers with no ego-to-ego edge — Refuge does not calibrate Anchor, Critic does — so the only serial chain in the architecture runs through the shadow arc. The shadow arc is the transmission bus; the ego arc is the target set. And because shadow output is Unvalued and dismissed, every transmission step is invisible to the person: the descent is experienced as a series of unexplained arrivals.

What is recalibrated differs by receiver — Refuge's recovery, Anchor's standard, Lead's attentional default, Flood's trigger set — but the operation is uniform. Falsifier: any position capturing out of this order (Critic before Refuge, Gamble before Anchor, Anchor with Refuge still sovereign) kills the rule.

## 2. Immediate and accumulation captures

Captures come in two kinds, and which kind a position undergoes is fixed by whether it reads its partner directly or samples its partner's pattern.

Shadow captures alternate. **Counter** monitors Hunger's signal directly — immediate; one fixation-shaped signal suffices. **Critic** samples Refuge's behavioral pattern — accumulation; a majority-corrupted sample is needed before its updates go fixation-shaped, and Critic is doing its job correctly throughout. **Gamble** reads Anchor's standard as its comparator — immediate; it flips when the standard crosses a threshold and nothing accumulates at Gamble itself. **Flood** evaluates its trigger on Lead-filtered perception — accumulation; the override entrains only after repeated firings on fixation-defined emergencies.

Ego captures: **Hunger** is a discrete identification event and the seed of all downstream quantization. **Refuge, Anchor, Lead** are accumulation captures with a discrete crossing at the end.

Predicted transition profile: sharp at Levels 2 and 3, slow through 4 and 5, two sharp crossings at 6 and 7 separated by a slow interval, slow through 8 and 9. Boundary establishes fast (early childhood), the average range is slow (where most adults sit for decades), Terminal is progressive (breakdown, not event). That this matches the Riso-Hudson clinical picture was not designed in. Falsifier: if retrospective accounts show Levels 3 or 7 as slow drifts, or 5 or 9 as abrupt, the read/sample distinction is wrong.

## 3. Four stages = four nested pairs

Each stage is one nested pair sealing, ego position first. Stages are chained by the shadow→ego edges.

| Stage | Pair sealed | Levels | What is lost when the pair seals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boundary | Hunger + Counter | 2–3 | The boundary between self and fixation |
| Maintenance | Refuge + Critic | 4–5 | Self-sustaining capacity: recovery and recalibration |
| Reality-testing | Anchor + Gamble | 6–7 | Internal verification: the standard and the discrepancy detector |
| Terminal | Lead + Flood | 8–9 | Both reality-contact channels |

Level 1 is no position captured. The stage partition is the same in every part of this document; the OLA coaching bands in Part III are an overlay that tracks Lead's status and is permitted to split Reality-testing.

## 4. The sequence, by mechanism

**Level 2 — Hunger, identification.** Not corruption. Awareness shifts from observing the reach to identifying with it; the fixation is now defended as the self's legitimate interest. Discrete. Amplitude is set by substrate pressure. Riso-Hudson Level 2 is Psychological Capacity, where the basic fear first arises — the two frameworks independently locate the origin at the same level with the same content.

**Level 3 — Counter, immediate.** Counter defends motivated striving by monitoring Hunger's output; the first fixation-shaped signal captures it. Counter is Strong + Unvalued, so captured Counter is proficient defense of the fixation's territory applied with a real standard: the golden rule, symmetric, principled pushback. The first inter-stage edge opens: Counter's threat output is adjacent to Refuge and begins bleeding into it. Refuge is under load but not captured — downtime is repurposed toward fixation-relevant activity, output rises, restoration still works. This is Riso-Hudson Social Value decomposed into two structural facts: Counter captured gives the principled defense, Refuge encroached gives the productivity. Falsifier: the Level 3 output increase should be sourced from downtime, not from efficiency or longer deliberate hours; and Level 2 should show lower output with cleaner restoration.

**Level 4 — Refuge, accumulation.** Sustained Counter threat output plus Hunger's reaching contaminate both of Refuge's modes. Propulsion becomes fixation-shaped forward motion; recovery stops restoring. Capture is the point where rest no longer produces clean output: the person cannot decompress out of the fixation, and "off mode" looks like "on mode" at lower intensity. Riso-Hudson Social Role — the gift becomes the thing that cannot be set down — is the same event.

**Level 5 — Critic, accumulation.** Critic has been sampling Refuge's unguarded pattern all along, because that is how it calibrates Anchor; the sample is now corrupted. Once it is majority-corrupted, Critic's updates to Anchor go fixation-shaped. Surface: Critic's targets redirect from Anchor's native negative image to fixation concerns — the person audits others against what the fixation fears. Riso-Hudson Interpersonal Control. Write-back is now running corrupted updates continuously; the second inter-stage edge is open. Substrate pressure is the rate term on both captures in this stage: it amplifies the signal at Hunger, which amplifies Counter's output, which raises the bleed into Refuge, which raises the corruption fraction in Critic's sample.

**Level 6 — Anchor, accumulation with a gate flip.** Anchor's standard drifts continuously under corrupted updates. Capture is the first of two thresholds on that drift: the **gate flip**, where the fixation-shaped standard validates the majority of Lead's outputs. The leaden rule — a real standard, asymmetrically applied — and Riso-Hudson Overcompensation name the same asymmetry. The Anchor trap: colonized Anchor is not passively contaminated, it actively produces fixation-shaped stability that looks healthy, because stabilizing is its job. Healthy Anchor stabilizes across domains; colonized Anchor stabilizes one thing obsessively. Fixation utility for Lead (§5) begins determining surface presentation here, and the stakes-distribution diagnostic reads directly off the gate.

**Level 7 — Gamble, immediate at the second threshold.** Gamble is a discrepancy detector whose comparator is Anchor's standard, with no independent reference. The second threshold on Anchor's drift is **discrepancy inversion**: the standard is corrupt enough that Flood's *clean* data reads as deviant. It is higher than the gate flip because Lead's outputs are already partly fixation-shaped by Level 5 and are cheap to validate, whereas Flood's data is raw until Terminal and is expensive to read as wrong — reading corrupted output as acceptable takes less drift than reading clean input as wrong. When the second threshold is crossed, Gamble's polarity inverts in the same step: the flash now fires on deviation from the fixation and pulls the self back. Nothing accumulates at Gamble; Level 7 is a genuine state (Gamble inverted, Lead not yet entrained) whose duration is set by how long Lead capture takes. The last internal check is gone; the system is unfalsifiable from inside. Riso-Hudson Violation is the behavioral face of this epistemic fact — violation becomes available once nothing internal can flag it. Prediction: in high-utility configurations the gate flips early, so the 6→7 gap is wide; in low-utility configurations the gate flip already required deep drift, so the gap is narrow. Falsifier: if Gamble surprise vanishes simultaneously with leaden-rule onset in all configurations, the two thresholds are one and Level 7 is not a distinct level.

**Level 8 — Lead, accumulation (attentional entrainment).** The third inter-stage edge: Gamble is the interrupt line into Lead's attention, and after Level 7 every interrupt is fixation-shaped. Lead resists longest on the ego side because it is directable and externally corrected. Substrate pressure is a co-condition here, not only a rate term: escalating IV-territory threat narrows attention to the threatened domain and consumes deliberate direction with threat monitoring, and the vacuum fills with captured Gamble's interrupts. Lead — Strong, Valued, plastic — learns that fixation-relevant stimuli are the salient ones because those are the only ones it is pulled to. Capture is a fixation-shaped default orientation: perception pre-filtered before it reaches the self. Terminal onset. The person becomes maximally predictable — a stable attractor, a stereotype in the precise sense. Riso-Hudson Delusion and Compulsion. Tell: substrate stress narrows attention to the IV *domain*; Gamble-driven capture narrows it to fixation-*strategy* cues within that domain, in low-stakes contexts where the threat is absent. Both an SP-3 and an SP-6 under money threat attend to money; only the captured one attends specifically to what the money says about image, or specifically to who can be trusted with it, when no threat is present.

**Level 9 — Flood, accumulation (trigger-set entrainment).** Flood's trigger question — does this exceed deliberate processing? — is evaluated on Lead-filtered perception, so after Level 8 the emergencies are fixation-defined. Flood is still clean at Level 8 and still fires as self-protective inversion: the override removes the organism from perceived danger, in Hunger's domain, unfixated. Substrate pressure raises exceedance frequency; Flood fires repeatedly on corrupted triggers; the override trains to that set. Capture is when Flood no longer distinguishes fixation threat from organismic threat. The inversion persists; the *direction* of the override flips from away-from-danger to toward-fixation — the ENFP's Se-Flood stops being reckless sensory escape and becomes reckless sensory action in service of the image. Both input channels are sealed. Flood has no outward target. Riso-Hudson Pathological Destructiveness is the fixation at emergency intensity with the survival check removed. Falsifier: if Level 9 dysregulation still runs away from danger rather than toward the fixation, the Lead→Flood edge is wrong and Terminal is a two-stage event.

## 5. Fixation utility for Lead

The single surface modulator. The fixation evaluates each position's native output for utility relative to its strategy.

**High utility** — Lead's native output directly serves the fixation (Te-Lead × 3, Ni-Lead × 4, Se-Lead × 8): Lead passes the corrupted gate and is deployed in fixation service across all stakes. Integrated, ego-syntonic, recognizable as the type-fixation archetype; the fixation is visible *in* Lead operation. Counter operation is minimal because Lead is doing the work. Family seeks help.

**Low utility** — Lead's native output structurally conflicts with the fixation (Fi-Lead × 3, Se-Lead × 5): Lead fails validation in fixation-evaluation-hot contexts and operates only where the gate relaxes — private reflection, intimate trusted contexts, leisure, specific creative outlets. Counter handles high-stakes load through its existing Strong + Unvalued proficiency. Compressed, ego-dystonic, often mistyped because Counter-dominant operation resembles another type; the person senses the gap and seeks help. Equilibrium stabilizes at Reality-testing with Gamble intact because further colonization adds no marginal utility.

**Mixed utility** — context-dependent operation revealing the utility seam.

The formerly separate "compressed" and "degraded" presentations, and every retired exile etiology, are surface variants of this one modulator on the one mechanism. **Stakes-distribution diagnostic:** where does the typed Lead actually operate natively? Across all stakes → high utility. Moderate stakes only → mixed. Low stakes only → low utility. Nowhere observable → extreme corruption or typing error. Falsifier: a configuration with high-utility Lead presenting as compressed, or low-utility presenting as integrated at the same depth, breaks the modulator.

## 6. Gamble's three properties

**Sensitivity** — the threshold a background signal must cross to surface. Feedback-modulated: Gamble is Unvalued, its interrupts are dismissed, and repeated dismissal raises the threshold. Through the average range, as the ego circuit runs increasingly fixation-shaped and credits Gamble less, Gamble fires less. A gradient at Gamble from Level 4, independent of Anchor; why surprise becomes *rare* (5–6) before it becomes *absent* (7+). Falsifier: if crediting hunches does not raise firing frequency at Levels 5–6, sensitivity is not feedback-modulated.

**Lens** — the function Gamble evaluates through, Refuge's in opposing attitude. Positional protection holds for the *data* (Flood's stream is raw until Terminal) but not for the *framing*: from Level 4 the shared function is run fixation-shaped most of the day at Refuge, and the vocabulary Gamble surfaces in leaks toward the fixation's. The flash still catches what is wrong and still reads it as wrong; the frame is increasingly the captured engine's. Falsifier: if the framing of surfacings does not shift toward the fixation's vocabulary between 4 and 7, positional protection holds in full.

**Polarity** — the comparator, Anchor's standard. Discrete, at the second threshold (§4, Level 7). Because polarity corruption is in the comparator and not the signal, a surfacing at Level 7 or 8 still contains the accurate discrepancy. The person cannot read it. Someone else can.

Unsummonable stands: the background channel cannot be queried. Unimprovable is withdrawn: sensitivity is trainable in both directions, so hit rate and credited fraction can improve even though directability cannot.

## 7. Substrate pressure, three roles

Rate term on every accumulation capture, via Hunger signal → Counter threat output → downstream. Co-condition at Lead, where it consumes deliberate direction. Exceedance-frequency driver at Flood and at Gamble's firing rate — which after Level 7 means more accurate flashes read through the wrong lens, each confirming the fixation with data that was true. Managing substrate pressure is a direct intervention on write-back rate and is the first step of the clinical sequence.

Three amplifiers of pressure produce the same mechanism at different rates and are not separate etiologies. **Punishment:** environmental attack shapes decompression; the person stops producing punished outputs even at rest; event-traceable. **Overwhelm:** the relational environment is calibrated to a stronger party's output and genuine decompression cannot occur; gradual. **Constitutional dissonance:** low utility for Lead means the fixation's own operation invalidates Lead's native output; internal, no external agent required, and the only one that gives the fixation an active incentive to maintain depth. **Cult environments** compress the developmental timeline from years to months by delivering Refuge domination and direct Lead suppression through the same relational vehicle, with demand escalation tuned to keep write-back rate high; shadow positions capture automatically as thresholds are crossed and defend the doctrine as fixation territory.

# Part III — Assessment

## 1. What you are reading

Operating level is a state, not a trait. It shifts week to week within a range whose ceiling is set by accumulated write-back; the current read is where the person is now, the range is what recovery has to walk back. Energy state is orthogonal: a depleted person at Level 4 and a rested person at Level 4 present differently but are at the same depth. Read depth from which positions are captured, not from how bad the day is.

Every observation is of output. For introverts the visible output is Anchor-shaped; for extraverts it is Lead-shaped. The signals that bypass the Anchor and read the architecture directly are what the person pushes back on (Counter — reveals Lead's domain), what they dismiss in others (Critic — reveals the fixation), and what surprises them about their own competence (Gamble — reveals the shadow stack).

## 2. Coaching bands

The bands are an overlay on the four stages that tracks Lead's status, which is why Reality-testing splits.

**Boundary (1–3).** Recovery, stabilization, and perception are sovereign. The person can observe their own patterns. Coaching works directly: name what you see and they can hear it. Golden rule applies; pushback is principled and they recover.

**Maintenance (4–6).** Refuge through Anchor captured; Lead still perceives independently. Coaching works indirectly — naming the fixation directly gets reframed by the maintenance layer; work through Lead's domain instead. Gamble surprise becomes rare across this band as sensitivity is suppressed; polarity is still native. Re-sensitization (Part IV §3) is viable and is a prerequisite for growth work.

**Reality-testing (7).** Gamble inverted; Lead still perceives independently. Coaching through Lead reaches perception, but the person cannot register discrepancy internally. Intervention is external registration: reread the person's own Gamble surfacings for them — the data is accurate, the reading is not. Not yet a management problem.

**Terminal (8–9).** Lead and Flood captured. No reality-contact channel is independent. Coaching doesn't work. Management problem: protect the team, set hard boundaries, escalate.

## 3. Diagnostic table

| Observation | Inferred level | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Self-aware humor about own patterns; feedback lands without defensiveness | 1–2 | High |
| Pushback at fixation territory but recovers; decompresses cleanly; golden rule | 3 | High |
| Output up, sourced from downtime; restoration intact | 3 | Medium — distinguishes from 4 by restoration |
| "Off mode" looks like "on mode" at lower intensity; no fixation-free gear | 4+ | Medium — need Critic/Anchor data |
| Consistent Critic targets mapping to fixation concerns | 5+ | High for fixation ID; medium for level |
| Surprise competence and Gamble surprise rare but present; person dismisses own hunches | 5–6 | Medium — polarity native: hunches still read as anomalies when they occur |
| Stabilizer behavior rigid and fixation-shaped; leaden rule; stability serves the fixation | 6 | High |
| Native Lead operating only at low stakes; Counter carrying high stakes | 6+, low utility | High for utility; medium for level |
| Cannot be surprised by counter-evidence; Gamble surprise absent; hunches confirm the fixation | 7 | High |
| Attention narrows to fixation-strategy cues in low-stakes contexts; doesn't register disconfirming input | 8 | High |
| Dysregulation is fixation-shaped; override runs toward the fixation rather than away from danger | 9 | High — also highest-fidelity stack read |

The leaden rule at 6 and the absence of surprise at 7 are the two crossings on Anchor's drift. Their lag is the utility prediction in Part II §4: wide in integrated presentations, narrow in compressed ones.

## 4. Real-time reading

Three activations are visible in conversation and each names a position.

**Reaching** — Hunger acute activation, Counter co-active. Enneagram flavor plus Counter's threat texture. Don't satisfy the reach; don't deploy growth-direction content.

**Gripping** — Refuge active under fixation load. The goal is clean Refuge output, not just decompression; reduce fixation pressure.

**Rejecting** — Counter active. Don't engage the Counter directly; reach past it privately.

Shadow positions appear on their own schedule. Critic appears continuously in commentary about others; three or four consistent targets is a high-confidence Anchor read, since Critic's targets are Anchor's negative image. Gamble appears episodically and involuntarily; a single appearance is suggestive and the person's own surprise at it confirms it. Flood appears only in full dysregulation; the personality inversion is unmistakable and is the most direct stack read available.

IV domain is read from the threat register. SP: what is actually stable or real; worst case is losing the job, the money, the health, the roof. SX: the specific person; worst case is bond rupture. SO: what happened in front of the room; worst case is public dismissal. The IV disambiguates which substrate pressure is running, and therefore which pressure reduction comes first.

## 5. The odd/even signature

Odd levels (3, 5, 7, 9) are shadow captures; their output is Unvalued and dismissed. Even levels (4, 6, 8) are ego captures; they are experienced as self. So transitions into odd levels should be reported as *the world changing* — people got more hostile, more careless, more dangerous — and transitions into even levels as *the self changing* — I've been different, I can't relax the way I used to. Riso-Hudson's scalar driver predicts no alternation. Falsifier: if descent narratives are uniformly self-referential or uniformly world-referential, the alternation is an artifact of the position model. Consequence for intervention: only what the person can perceive as theirs is addressable, which is even-level positions and Gamble's registration.

## 6. Typing errors the mechanism predicts

Low-utility configurations are mistyped as the type whose ego stack their Counter-dominant operation resembles; check where native Lead operates at low stakes. Introverts are typed from Anchor-shaped output; use Counter and Critic to reach Lead. Flood is misattributed to Lead when the two share attitude; the Flood signature is unattended and disowned. Aspirational self-typing selects the growth direction's type; the tell is that the claimed Lead never appears under load. A person at Level 8 is typeable from fixation and passion alone and the stack is nearly invisible, which is itself the diagnosis.

# Part IV — Intervention

## 1. Growth direction as Counter-prediction falsification

Hunger's scarcity model is the fixation's account of what is dangerously absent and what arrives if the strategy fails. Counter's threat output is sourced from that model and fires in the texture of the function at position 5 (Appendix B). The growth direction is the specific experience Counter's threat output declares will not arrive — not a better quality, not an aspirational practice, the exact outcome Counter identifies as the consequence of the fixation failing (Appendix C). Each genuine growth-direction experience contradicts Counter's signal by delivering what it said was impossible, and revises the scarcity model's *parameters* — how bad, how likely, how soon — by one data point. The structure is untouched; the fixation remains. What changes is calibration, and calibration is what drives level.

Two requirements follow, and they are distinct. **Sourcing** must be external. Counter's threat output is oriented precisely toward what the growth direction delivers, so approaching it deliberately activates the defense; performing the growth direction is the fixation operating. **Registration** is internal and runs through Gamble, the discrepancy detector. Counter says X will not arrive; X arrives; something has to fire "that does not fit." Lead perceives X, but Lead's output passes through Anchor's gate, which may not validate X as significant. Gamble is what registers it. Below Level 7 the person registers falsification themselves. At Level 7 and above the comparator is inverted, so the external party must supply the reading as well as the experience.

## 2. Recovery bootstraps through Gamble

The first falsifications cannot register cleanly because the registration organ is degraded on three axes — sensitivity suppressed, lens leaked, and at 7+ polarity inverted. This is why they must be external and repeated, and why the external party supplies the reading. Each one that lands lowers Counter's output slightly; Refuge cleanses slightly; the lens cleans because it leaks from Refuge; crediting the interrupts that arrive lowers the sensitivity threshold; the next falsification registers a little more internally. Anchor recalibrates against the accumulating clean Refuge data, proportional to the write-back accumulated over the fixation's operational lifetime. Once Anchor's drift recrosses the second threshold, polarity restores and Gamble is an internal check again; the person is below Level 7 and can carry the rest.

Recovery runs the descent's edges in reverse, but not symmetrically. Only the accumulation captures — Refuge, Critic, Anchor, Lead, Flood — have a gradient to walk back. The immediate captures — Counter, Gamble — restore the moment their partner does. So recovery is slow through the stages and sharp at their shadow-side boundaries, the mirror of the descent profile in Part II §2.

When colonization recedes and Lead operation expands into broader stakes-contexts, the returning capacity initially expresses through the fixation's filter, which is harder to detect than Counter-dominant operation was. Do not read early Lead return as integration.

## 3. Clinical sequence

1. **Substrate pressure reduction.** Identify the first instinct's territory and reduce the threat in it. This is a direct intervention on write-back rate and precedes everything. Minimal growth-direction viability — one externally sourced experience that lands — is needed before pressure intervention can proceed fully, because the fixation resists pressure reduction as loss of the emergency it manages.
2. **Gamble re-sensitization.** Get the person crediting the interrupts they already receive before supplying new experiences: treat hunches, surprise competence, and off-pattern flashes as data rather than dismissing them. Trains hit rate and credited fraction; does not and cannot train directability.
3. **Growth introduction, externally sourced and repeated.** Arrange conditions under which the growth direction can arrive; do not instruct the person to perform it. At Level 7+ pair every experience with an external reading.
4. **Anchor recalibration** proceeds on its own once clean Refuge data accumulates; the practitioner's job is to keep steps 1–3 running and to not mistake fixation-filtered Lead return for the end.

Substrate pressure reduction before growth introduction; re-sensitization between them; externally sourced first experience; never self-performed.

## 4. Two failure modes of premature growth work

Growth-direction content applied too early fails in two distinguishable ways and they take different corrections.

**Fixation simulation** (Level 7 and above). Polarity is inverted and the fixation is running at depth. The initial experience is real and Counter is briefly contradicted, but the fixation reads the growth direction as a resource and runs its native strategy on the growth direction's content — the 3 performs supported non-performance, the 6 makes vigilance out of rest, the 1 perfects pleasure. Active, recognizable, and it deepens the home fixation. Correction: stop, reduce substrate pressure.

**Non-registration** (Levels 4–6). Polarity is native but sensitivity is suppressed. The falsification arrives and simply does not register: inert, no simulation, no harm, no effect. Correction: re-sensitize, then try again.

Falsifier for the distinction: if premature growth work at Levels 4–6 produces recognizable simulation rather than inertia, the sensitivity/polarity split is wrong and Gamble has one property, not three.

## 5. Substrate pressure work by first instinct

SP-first: stabilize material and physical ground first — income, housing, health, schedule; the fixation is reading these as the emergency. SO-first: stabilize standing — a group, a role, a place at the table that does not depend on the fixation's performance. SX-first: stabilize the dyad — one reliable intense connection whose continuity does not track the fixation's strategy. In each case the target is not comfort but the removal of the specific threat the first instinct is monitoring, so that Hunger's signal amplitude drops and everything downstream runs at a lower rate.

## 6. Colonization work by stage

**Boundary.** Name the fixation and its defense directly; the person can hear it. Watch for Refuge encroachment — the output increase sourced from downtime — and protect restoration before it fails.

**Maintenance.** Work through Lead's domain; the maintenance layer reframes direct naming. Goal is clean Refuge output, which means fixation pressure reduction, not more rest. Re-sensitize Gamble. Critic's targets are the fixation map; use them to identify the scarcity model, not to confront the person with them.

**Reality-testing.** At 6, the stability is the symptom; do not trust Anchor-consistent behavior. At 7, external registration: the person's own sonar is still accurate, reread it for them.

**Terminal.** Management. Protect others; set boundaries; escalate. The person is typeable from fixation and passion alone, which is both the diagnosis and the reason coaching has nothing to work with.

Do not proceed to deep Anchor work while colonization is at Maintenance or deeper; the fixation captures returning instruments. Weaken colonization first so that returning Lead capacity has a clean gate to pass.

# Appendix F — Test set

Each is stated so that one clean observation decides it.

1. **Ordinality.** Any position captured out of sequence falsifies adjacency corruption.
2. **Read/sample profile.** Levels 3 and 7 abrupt, 5 and 9 gradual; the reverse falsifies the immediate/accumulation distinction.
3. **Two thresholds.** Gamble surprise vanishing simultaneously with leaden-rule onset in all configurations collapses Levels 6 and 7.
4. **Utility lag.** The 6→7 gap should be wide in integrated (high-utility) presentations and narrow in compressed (low-utility) ones.
5. **Level 3 sourcing.** Level 3 output gain comes from downtime, not efficiency; Level 2 shows lower output with cleaner restoration.
6. **Odd/even reporting.** Odd-level transitions narrated as the world changing, even-level as the self changing; uniform narration falsifies the alternation.
7. **Sensitivity.** Crediting hunches raises Gamble firing frequency at Levels 5–6.
8. **Lens.** Framing of Gamble surfacings shifts toward the fixation's vocabulary between Levels 4 and 7.
9. **Failure modes.** Premature growth work at 4–6 is inert; at 7+ it simulates. Simulation at 4–6 collapses the three properties to one.
10. **Lead entrainment tell.** Fixation-strategy attentional narrowing appears in low-stakes contexts at Level 8; IV-domain narrowing alone is substrate stress.
11. **Flood direction.** Level 9 override runs toward the fixation; away-from-danger dysregulation at 9 falsifies the Lead→Flood edge.
12. **Utility modulator.** High-utility Lead presenting compressed, or low-utility presenting integrated, at matched depth.
13. **Structure inborn.** MZ-apart concordance on type position and wing exceeds DZ; otherwise the structural claim fails.
14. **Stack constitutional.** Dominant function shows cross-situational consistency and MZ-apart concordance; high within-person variance in processing preference falsifies it.
